How to Order at Campus Bites

Hungry between classes? Follow these simple steps to order your favorite meals and enjoy fresh food without confusion.

Explore Our Menu
Four Easy Steps

Place Your Order

Getting your favorite food is easy with our simple process.

01
🍔

Choose Your Food

Check our menu and daily deals. Decide what you want to eat and confirm your selected items.

02
💳

Pay at the Counter

Tell the cashier your order, confirm the total price, and pay at the counter using an available payment method.

03
🎟️

Collect Your Token

Keep your order receipt or token safe. Listen for your token number or follow the counter's instructions.

04
🍽️

Pick Up Your Meal

When your order is ready, present your token at the pickup counter, collect your food, and enjoy your meal.

Plan Your Visit

Beat the Lunch Rush

Campus Bites can get busy when students arrive between classes. Allow extra time during busy periods so you can order and collect your food comfortably.

  • Visit earlier if you have a short break.
  • Decide what you want before reaching the counter.
  • Keep your payment ready to reduce waiting time.
  • Follow the queue and staff instructions.
⏰

Busy Periods

12:00 PM – 2:00 PM

Lunch hours may have longer queues. These are suggested peak hours; actual waiting times may vary.

A Smooth Experience

Ordering & Pickup Guidelines

A few simple rules help everyone enjoy a better cafeteria experience.

🎫

Keep Your Token

Keep your receipt or token until your order has been collected and checked.

👥

Respect the Queue

Wait your turn and avoid crowding the ordering and pickup counters.

✅

Check Your Order

Check your items before leaving the counter. Inform the staff politely if something is missing.

🧹

Keep It Clean

Dispose of wrappers and leftover waste in the designated bins after your meal.
